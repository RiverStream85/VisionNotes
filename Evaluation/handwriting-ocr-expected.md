Kinematics with constant acceleration:

$v = v_0 + at$

$x = x_0 + v_0 t + \frac{1}{2} a t^2$

$v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a(x - x_0)$

Example: a ball is thrown straight up at 12 m/s. How high does it go?

At the top $v = 0$, so $h = \frac{v_0^2}{2g} = \frac{144}{19.6} \approx 7.3$ m.

Remember: the acceleration is $-9.8$ m/s² the whole time, even at the top.

Energy check: $\frac{1}{2} m v_0^2 = m g h$, the mass cancels.
